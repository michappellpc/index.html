Pocket Flight Surgeon Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

Pocket Flight Surgeon is an offline aeromedical reference aid. The app does not require an account and does not collect, sell, transmit, or share personal data with the developer.

Data Stored on Device

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Birthday
  • Aviation role
  • Last flight physical type and date
  • Starred conditions
  • Packet checklist progress
  • First-run safety acknowledgement

This information is stored on-device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Calendar Reminders

The Apple Calendar option creates a local .ics calendar file and opens the iOS share/import sheet. The app does not access, read, or modify your calendar database directly.

The Google Calendar option opens a browser URL with reminder details prefilled. Use of Google Calendar is subject to Google's privacy policy and terms.

Health Data

The app does not use HealthKit and does not access health records, sensors, or medical data from other apps.

Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

The app does not use analytics, third-party tracking, or tracking SDKs.

External Links

The app may open external reference links or calendar links in your browser. External websites are governed by their own privacy policies.
